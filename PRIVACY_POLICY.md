# Privacy Policy for Last.

**Effective date:** September 8, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 8, 2026

Last. is a days-since tracker for the recurring things in your life. This
Privacy Policy explains how the developer of Last. ("we," "us," or "our")
handles information when you use the Last. iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, widget,
and Live Activity experiences (together, the "App").

## The short version

- Last. does not require you to create an account.
- Your Things, reset history, prints, preferences, and other personal content
  are stored on your device by default.
- Free users do not receive automatic cloud sync. If you have a verified paid
  membership and use Standard mode, Last. syncs your content through your
  private Apple CloudKit database so it is available on your supported devices.
- Anonymous analytics and diagnostics are off until you choose to allow them.
  You can change that choice in Settings at any time.
- On-device only mode pauses CloudKit sync. If you have allowed analytics, that
  choice remains separate from On-device only mode, as do the purchase checks
  required to verify paid features.
- Analytics and purchase services never receive your Thing names, custom text,
  reset dates, print images, or CloudKit identifiers from us.
- Last. does not sell your personal information, does not show ads at launch,
  does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not
  track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Information Last. handles

### Content you create

The App lets you create and manage information such as:

- Thing names and custom labels;
- reset dates and reset history, including corrections and undo status;
- selected colors and icons;
- nudge preferences;
- generated print recipes, print metadata, and favorites;
- archive status and other App settings; and
- a random print identity value used to keep generated artwork consistent.

This is your content. It is stored locally unless you use the private iCloud
sync described below or choose to export or share it.

The App does not request access to your contacts, calendars, microphone,
precise location, health data, or advertising identifier.

### Information stored on your devices

Last. stores your content and settings in its app container and, where needed,
in a shared App Group container used by the App's widgets. The App may send a
bounded copy of selected presentation data and reset commands between your
paired iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's Watch connectivity services.

Widgets, notifications, Apple Watch complications, and Live Activities can
display information outside the main App. Depending on the feature and your
settings, this may include a Thing name, its elapsed day count, recent history,
or print artwork. Anyone who can view your unlocked device, Lock Screen, Home
Screen, Apple Watch, or Dynamic Island may be able to see that information.
Last. provides generic or reduced-detail presentation in privacy-sensitive
states where supported, and lets you control relevant Live Activity name
display and notification settings.

### Private iCloud sync

Automatic sync is available only with verified paid access. When paid access
is active, you are signed in to iCloud, sync is not manually paused, and the App
is in Standard mode, Last. sends the following to Apple's CloudKit service:

- Things and their names, labels, colors, icons, settings, and archive state;
- reset records and their dates;
- generated print recipes and print favorites; and
- the random print identity value needed to reproduce your prints consistently
  on your devices.

This data is stored in the private database for Last. in your iCloud account.
Apple states that a user's private CloudKit database is available only to that
user by default and that its contents count toward the user's iCloud storage.
Last. does not use a public CloudKit database for this content and does not add
a feature for sharing this database with other users.

Free users do not receive automatic CloudKit sync. If a paid membership expires
or sync is paused, the App stops syncing but does not automatically delete your
local content or the existing copy in iCloud.

On-device only is a paid privacy control that pauses CloudKit sync. Turning it
on keeps your Last. content off iCloud from that device, but it does not delete
content already stored in iCloud. You can separately delete Last.'s cloud data
as described in **Your choices and deletion** below.

Apple processes iCloud and CloudKit information under [Apple's Privacy
Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### Purchases and membership verification

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your
payment-card or bank-account details.

Last. uses RevenueCat to retrieve available purchase options, validate App Store
transactions, restore purchases, prevent fraud, and determine whether premium
and cloud features should be available. RevenueCat may process:

- an anonymous RevenueCat App User ID;
- App Store receipt and purchase history, product, entitlement, subscription,
  trial, renewal, and expiration information;
- App and device technical information needed to operate the service; and
- service-request information such as IP address and request time.

Last. disables RevenueCat's automatic device-identifier collection and does not
send RevenueCat your name, email address, Thing names, custom text, reset
history, precise personal timestamps, print images, or CloudKit identifiers.
RevenueCat may still create an anonymous identifier needed to associate purchase
status with this installation. Purchase and entitlement checks continue in
On-device only mode so the App can restore purchases and enforce paid access.

RevenueCat processes this information for us under its [Privacy
Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy-policy) and applicable data-processing
terms.

### Privacy-filtered analytics and diagnostics

Last. asks before collecting or sending anonymous analytics and diagnostics.
If you choose **Allow anonymous analytics**, Last. uses TelemetryDeck to
understand whether features work, measure broad product usage, and diagnose
reliability and performance problems. If you choose **Not now**, collection
remains off. Whenever you turn analytics off, Last. stops collecting and
sending new product analytics and diagnostics and deletes signals still
waiting to be sent.

When enabled, Last. sends only a closed set of events and values, such as:

- App opens and broad screen or feature interactions;
- coarse count ranges, rather than the names of Things;
- selected built-in color, icon, poster style, widget family, or feature type;
- closed result categories for resets, shares, notifications, purchases, sync,
  and Live Activities; and
- privacy-filtered performance and diagnostic measurements such as launch time,
  memory use, animation hitch ratios, hangs, and crashes.

These signals can include a one-way hash of a randomly generated installation
identifier and a random session identifier. We use them only to distinguish
anonymous installations or sessions for product and reliability analysis. We
do not connect them to a name, email address, Apple Account, RevenueCat customer
record, advertising profile, or other directly identifying information.

Analytics and diagnostics do **not** include Thing names, custom text, search
text, reset dates, other precise personal timestamps, print images, CloudKit
record identifiers, advertising identifiers, exact purchase revenue, or raw
provider identifiers. Last. does not use analytics for advertising or tracking
across apps or websites.

TelemetryDeck states that it does not store IP addresses and that its app
analytics uses anonymized identifiers. TelemetryDeck processes these signals
under its [Privacy Policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/) and explains its
App data practices in its [Privacy FAQ](https://telemetrydeck.com/docs/guides/privacy-faq/).

After you allow them, privacy-filtered analytics and diagnostics continue in
On-device only mode unless you turn them off. That mode controls CloudKit
synchronization, not these limited service signals. You can change the
analytics choice separately at any time.

### Notifications

Nudges, optional ideas, and optional membership reminders are scheduled locally
on your device using Apple's notification system. Last. does not use a push
notification server. Notification permission is requested when you enable a
feature that needs it, and optional ideas and membership reminders are off by
default.

A Thing nudge may display the Thing's name in a local notification. The App's
analytics may record only the notification's closed category and whether it was
scheduled or opened; it does not receive the Thing name or notification text.

### Photos, exports, archives, and sharing

When you choose to save a print or reveal video to Photos, Last. requests
permission to add that item to your photo library. The App does not request
permission to browse your photo library.

When you export a `.lastarchive`, Last. creates a user-controlled backup that
contains your Things, reset history, print recipes, favorites, and random print
identity value. CloudKit account and record identifiers are not included. When
you share a print, video, or archive, it is sent to the destination you select
through Apple's share interface.

Files you save, export, or share are then controlled by you, the selected
recipient, and the selected storage or sharing service. Their handling is
governed by those parties' privacy policies, not this one.

### Support communications

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as
your email address, message, and any screenshots or files you attach. We use it
to respond, troubleshoot, protect the App, and keep appropriate support records.
Please do not include sensitive personal content unless it is necessary for us
to help you.

## How we use information

We use information only to:

- provide, maintain, and secure the App;
- save, display, synchronize, export, and restore features you request;
- process purchases and verify access to paid features;
- provide local notifications you enable;
- understand feature use in aggregate;
- diagnose crashes, hangs, performance issues, errors, abuse, or fraud;
- respond to support requests; and
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not use your personal Last. content to advertise to you, build an
advertising profile, or train a generative artificial-intelligence model.

## When information is disclosed

We disclose information only in these circumstances:

- **Service providers.** Apple provides the App Store, CloudKit, Photos,
  notifications, widgets, Watch connectivity, and Live Activities. RevenueCat
  provides purchase and entitlement infrastructure. TelemetryDeck provides
  privacy-focused analytics processing. These providers may process information
  only as needed to provide their services to us and under their applicable
  terms and privacy commitments.
- **At your direction.** We send exports and shared items to the person, app, or
  service you select.
- **Legal and safety reasons.** We may disclose information when reasonably
  necessary to comply with law, valid legal process, or enforceable government
  requests; protect rights, safety, and security; or investigate fraud or abuse.
- **Business transfer.** If responsibility for Last. is transferred as part of
  a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets,
  information may transfer subject to this policy and applicable law.

We require third-party processors acting on our behalf to protect information
consistently with this policy and applicable law. Apple also handles information
under the privacy terms that apply to the Apple services you choose to use.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for
cross-context behavioral advertising. Last. contains no active third-party ad
SDK or ad request at launch.

## Retention

- **Local App content** remains on your device until you delete content where
  the App provides that control, delete the App, or erase the device. Deleting
  the App does not necessarily delete an existing iCloud copy or files you
  previously exported.
- **CloudKit content** remains in your private iCloud database until you delete
  Last.'s cloud data, manage it through Apple where available, or Apple deletes
  it under its policies. A membership expiring does not automatically delete
  it.
- **Analytics and diagnostics while enabled** may remain temporarily queued on the
  device while offline and are removed from the local queue after successful
  delivery. Turning analytics off deletes signals still waiting to be sent.
  TelemetryDeck may retain anonymized event data according to its retention
  practices. Because we do not connect this data to your identity, we may not be
  able to locate an individual event in response to a personal-data request.
- **Purchase records** are retained by Apple and RevenueCat as needed to provide
  purchase history, restore access, prevent fraud, meet accounting or legal
  duties, and resolve disputes, under their applicable retention policies.
- **Support messages** are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to resolve
  the request, maintain appropriate records, and meet legal obligations.

We may retain aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be
used to identify you.

## Your choices and deletion

You can control your information in the following ways:

- **Cloud sync:** A verified paid member can turn on On-device only to pause
  CloudKit sync, or manually pause sync where the App offers that control.
- **Delete cloud data:** In Last.'s Settings, choose **Delete cloud data** to
  permanently delete Last.'s Things, reset history, prints, and favorites from
  its private CloudKit zone. This keeps the local copy and pauses sync on that
  device. Another device with sync still active could upload its local copy
  again, so pause sync on other devices as well if you want the deletion to
  remain complete.
- **Local data:** Delete content using available App controls, or delete the App
  to remove its local app container. User-created exports must be deleted from
  the location where you saved or shared them.
- **Notifications:** Disable individual reminder categories in Last.'s Settings
  or change Last.'s notification permission in system Settings.
- **Live Activities and visible names:** Change the relevant settings in Last.
  or end active Live Activities.
- **Anonymous analytics:** Anonymous analytics remains off until you allow it
  on the first-launch explanation or in **Settings → Anonymous analytics**.
  You can turn it off or back on at any time. Turning it off stops new
  analytics and diagnostic collection and deletes signals waiting to be sent.
  It does not affect App features, purchase verification, or content already
  processed in de-identified form.
- **Photos:** Change Last.'s Photos permission in system Settings.
- **Purchases:** Restore purchases in Last. and manage subscriptions using
  Apple's subscription-management tools.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction,
deletion, restriction, portability, or objection regarding personal information
we control, or to appeal a privacy-request decision. You may also have the right
to complain to your local privacy or data-protection authority. Contact us using
the details below. We may need enough information to verify and fulfill your
request, but Last. has no developer account system and some data is held only on
your device, in your private iCloud database, or in de-identified form that we
cannot associate with you.

## Security and international processing

We use technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the
information, including Apple's protected app storage, private CloudKit storage,
encrypted network connections, bounded service payloads, and separation of
personal App content from analytics and purchase attributes. No method of
storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute
security.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the one
where you live. Where required, we and our providers use recognized safeguards
for international transfers. See the linked provider policies for their
locations and transfer practices.

## Children

Last. is not directed to children under 13, or the higher minimum age required
in a child's jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect directly identifying
personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided such
information to us, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.
